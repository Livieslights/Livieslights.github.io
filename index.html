Behind the Lights

hi, I'm Livie! 💗

I'm a creative marketer and community-focused storyteller who loves turning ideas into things people actually want to see, share and connect with.

My interests live somewhere between social media, psychology, events, design, storytelling and meaningful community work.

Throughout high school, I was actively involved in volunteering and community service. I continue to build connections through local organizations and initiatives, including partnering with Volunteer Lubbock.

I believe good marketing shouldn't just look pretty. It should make people feel something.
